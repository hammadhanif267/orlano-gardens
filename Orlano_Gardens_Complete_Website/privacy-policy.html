01

Overview

Orlano Gardens provides remote digital outdoor design services and publishes garden-planning content. This policy applies when you browse the website, submit an inquiry, upload project information, use site features or contact us.

Orlano Gardens is responsible for the personal information described here unless another service, such as Etsy, explains that it controls its own processing.

Scope of this notice

This website and direct communications are covered. External platforms maintain separate privacy notices.

02

Information we may collect

The information collected depends on how you use the website. It may include:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, social username and preferred contact method.
  • Project location, measurements, style preferences, maintenance level, budget direction and keep-or-remove instructions.
  • Photos, walkthrough videos, plans, reference images and messages you choose to submit.
  • Technical data such as IP address, device and browser information, page activity, approximate location and cookie identifiers when the relevant tools are enabled.
  • Communication records, consent choices and information needed to manage a service request.
03

How information is used

Personal information may be used to respond to inquiries, determine project scope, create and deliver digital design concepts, maintain service records, protect the website, comply with legal duties and improve content. Non-essential marketing is used only when a valid permission or other lawful basis exists.

04

Legal bases and consent

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on steps requested before a contract, performance of a service contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations or consent. The basis depends on the purpose and location of the user.

Consent choices

Where consent is required for non-essential cookies, personalized advertising or marketing, you may refuse or withdraw it without affecting the core inquiry service.

05

Project photos, videos and creative files

Project media is used to understand the site and create the requested digital design. Submit only material you are entitled to share. Remove faces, license plates, house numbers or personal details that are not required.

Client-supplied property photos and videos are not published in the portfolio, articles, advertising or social media without separate permission.

06

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Essential storage may support security and privacy choices. If analytics or advertising services are enabled, those providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to measure activity, limit repetition, detect fraud or select ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this or other websites. Where required, a consent-management tool must allow visitors to accept, reject or adjust non-essential choices before those technologies operate.

Current build status

This downloadable static build does not install analytics or advertising scripts. The policy must be synchronized with the final production setup.

07

Etsy orders and payments

Orlano Gardens does not process full payment-card details on this website. Checkout and payment are completed through the official Etsy shop after scope confirmation. Etsy collects and uses order and payment information under its own privacy terms.

08

Sharing, providers and international transfers

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with providers supporting hosting, forms, email, storage, security, analytics, advertising or service delivery; with professional advisers; or when required by law. Personal information is not sold as an ordinary business product.

Providers may process information in different countries. Appropriate transfer safeguards should be used where required.

09

Data retention

Information should be kept only as long as reasonably necessary for inquiry handling, service delivery, support, security, accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations. Retention depends on the record type and platform. Information no longer required should be deleted or de-identified securely.

10

Your privacy rights

Depending on location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, data portability, withdrawal of consent or an opt-out from certain sale, sharing or targeted-advertising uses.

To make a request, email orlanogardens@gmail.com. Reasonable information may be requested to identify the relevant inquiry or order. Rights are not absolute and may be limited by law.

11

Children’s privacy

The website and custom design service are not directed to children under 13, and Orlano Gardens does not knowingly request personal information from them. Contact us if you believe a child submitted information.

12

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures should be used to protect information. No website, email or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers or sensitive data through project forms.

13

Third-party links

The website may link to Etsy, Instagram, Facebook, Google services or other external sites. Their practices are controlled by their own notices and settings.

14

Policy changes and contact

This policy may change when website features, providers, advertising practices or legal requirements change. Questions or privacy requests: orlanogardens@gmail.com.

Implementation requirement

Before public launch, replace assumptions with the real hosting, form, analytics, advertising, storage and consent configuration. A qualified professional should review the final legal text for the countries served.
