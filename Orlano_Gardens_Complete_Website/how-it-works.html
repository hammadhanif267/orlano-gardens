Step 01 / Project inputs

Show the whole problem—not one attractive angle.

Incomplete views produce weak assumptions. The design needs enough context to read connections, fixed features and scale.

  • 1–3 clear wide photographs from useful angles
  • A slow walking video when areas connect
  • Exact city, state/province and country
  • Measurements or a plan when scale is unclear
  • What must stay, move or be removed
  • Style, budget direction and maintenance preference
  • Known sun, shade, drainage and utility conditions
Input example

Crop private details that the design does not need. Client media is not published without permission.

Step 03 / Concept development

Several directions. One controlled decision.

Each concept stays tied to the submitted property. Differences should test meaningful choices such as planting structure, material balance, layout and focal emphasis.

  • Two or four concepts according to package
  • Clearly labeled AI-assisted digital visualization
  • Site-faithful architecture and fixed features
  • Practical scale and design reasoning

Step 04 / Final handoff

A useful next step—not a disappearing image.

  • Final selected concept direction
  • Plant and material list
  • Layout and placement guidance
  • Simple setup and basic care notes
  • One focused revision within purchased scope
