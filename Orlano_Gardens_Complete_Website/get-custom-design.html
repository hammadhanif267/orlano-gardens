Before you begin

Prepare the right evidence.

  • 1–3 wide horizontal photos from useful angles
  • A slow walking video for connected areas
  • Exact city, state/province and country
  • Measurements when scale is not clear
  • Style, maintenance and budget direction
  • Keep, remove and must-include notes

Photos stay private by default.

Client-supplied photos and video are used to understand the property. They are not published without separate permission.
