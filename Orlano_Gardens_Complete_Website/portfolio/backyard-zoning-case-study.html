Zone strategy
The design decision

Build around the center—not through it.

The strongest move was to resist adding features to every available patch of lawn.

  • Dining stays close to the house.
  • A secondary lounge creates a different use.
  • Planting frames rather than consumes the yard.
  • Circulation runs along clear, direct routes.
What the layout solves

Each zone earns its place.

Outdoor rooms are defined by use, connection and realistic movement—not decoration alone.

01

02

03

Perimeter planting

Planting softens edges, supports privacy and protects open ground.
