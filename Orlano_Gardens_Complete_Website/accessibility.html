01

Commitment

Orlano Gardens aims to make the website usable by people with different devices, input methods, vision, hearing and cognitive needs.

02

Accessibility features

  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, filters, forms and accordions
  • Visible focus indicators
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Responsive layouts and readable contrast
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Print-friendly article and legal pages
03

Known limitations

Some detailed project imagery may contain complex visual information that cannot be fully described in a short text alternative. Contact Orlano Gardens for a plain-language description of a specific concept.

04

Compatibility

The site is designed for current versions of major browsers and common assistive technologies. Very old browsers may not support the cookie dialog or every enhancement, but core links and content remain available.

05

Accessibility feedback

If you cannot access information or complete a task, email orlanogardens@gmail.com. Include the page, problem, device or assistive technology and the format you need.

06

Ongoing review

Accessibility must be checked again after the production host, third-party forms, advertising, analytics or embedded content are added. Those services can introduce barriers not present in this static build.

Implementation requirement

Before public launch, replace assumptions with the real hosting, form, analytics, advertising, storage and consent configuration. A qualified professional should review the final legal text for the countries served.
