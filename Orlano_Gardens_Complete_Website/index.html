Why design first

Remove the expensive guesswork.

A plant list alone cannot show scale, rhythm, circulation or how materials work together. A visual direction lets you compare those decisions before permanent work begins.

  • See the effect of mature scale and clearances.
  • Compare material and planting directions.
  • Protect windows, paths, drainage and utility access.
  • Give a local installer a clearer discussion starting point.
