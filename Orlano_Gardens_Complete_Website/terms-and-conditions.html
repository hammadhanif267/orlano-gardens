01

The service

Orlano Gardens provides remote digital outdoor design concepts and practical guidance based on client-supplied photos, measurements, location and preferences. The service does not include a property visit, physical landscaping, products, installation, engineering, permits or contractor services.

02

Scope confirmation

The client must provide the complete area before checkout when package fit is uncertain. The confirmed package controls the project area, concept count and included deliverables. A new or materially expanded area requires separate scope.

03

Client responsibilities

The client is responsible for accurate photos, measurements, location, keep-or-remove instructions and any known utilities, access, drainage or property restrictions. Orlano Gardens may pause work until missing information is supplied.

04

Payment and checkout

Payment is completed through the official Etsy shop. Etsy’s order, payment, cancellation and platform terms also apply. Do not send payment-card information through this website or email.

05

Delivery timing

Standard delivery is 3–5 working days after complete inputs and scope confirmation. The optional $29 priority add-on targets 24-hour delivery only when feasibility is confirmed before purchase. Delivery dates can change when required information is late or the agreed scope changes.

06

Concepts and revision

The package determines the number of concepts. One focused revision is included after one concept is selected. The revision refines that direction; it does not add a new area, replace the brief or create another package’s output.

07

Digital visualization boundary

Concept images are AI-assisted planning visualizations and are labeled Digital Design Concept. They are not construction drawings, measured plans, completed installations or guarantees of an exact physical result.

08

Local implementation

The client or a local professional must verify plant suitability, mature size, soil, utilities, drainage, property lines, permits, structure, fire clearances, product instructions, quantities and site conditions before work.

09

Use of deliverables

The client may use the delivered design for planning and implementation of the purchased property. Resale, redistribution, template extraction or commercial reuse outside the project requires written permission. Client photos remain the client’s material and are not published without separate permission.

10

Limitations

Design guidance depends on the accuracy and completeness of submitted information. Orlano Gardens cannot guarantee plant survival, contractor performance, product availability, code approval, cost, exact visual matching or physical construction outcomes.

11

Changes and contact

Terms may be updated for service, platform or legal changes. Questions should be sent to orlanogardens@gmail.com before purchase.

Implementation requirement

Before public launch, replace assumptions with the real hosting, form, analytics, advertising, storage and consent configuration. A qualified professional should review the final legal text for the countries served.
