01

What cookies are

Cookies and similar storage technologies are small records placed on a device or accessed by a website. Some support basic operation; others measure use or support advertising.

02

Current build status

This downloadable static build uses local storage only to remember cookie choices. It does not include live analytics or advertising code. The developer must update this policy when actual production tools are installed.

03

Essential storage

Essential storage remembers privacy preferences and may support security or core site operation. It cannot be disabled through the cookie panel because the choice itself must be remembered.

04

Analytics cookies

If enabled after valid consent, analytics tools may measure pages visited, interaction patterns, device and approximate location information. Do not activate this category until the provider, retention and consent rules are documented.

05

Advertising cookies

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may place or read cookies or use web beacons and IP addresses for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency control and measurement. Consent must be collected where required.

06

Managing choices

Use the cookie controls on this site to accept, reject or adjust optional categories. Browser settings may also block or delete storage, but doing so can remove remembered preferences.

07

Changes and contact

Review this policy whenever a new analytics, advertising, embedded-media or consent provider is installed. Questions: orlanogardens@gmail.com.

Implementation requirement

Before public launch, replace assumptions with the real hosting, form, analytics, advertising, storage and consent configuration. A qualified professional should review the final legal text for the countries served.
